## Posting tips

- **Post within the first hour** the thread is up (18:00–19:00 UTC on the 1st). Late comments are buried.
- **Format matters.** HN renders markdown loosely; use blank lines between sections, no bold, asterisks not always rendered.
- **Email and portfolio URL up top** — most readers scan, decide in 5 seconds.
- **Reply to anyone who comments on your top-level post** within 24 hours, even if they're just commenting (not pitching). HN is small, every reply earns goodwill.
- **Don't crosspost** to "Who is hiring?" thread same day. Keep them separate.
- **Track replies in your Obsidian vault.** A spreadsheet of "who replied to my HN comment, what they were building, did it convert" pays dividends month over month.
