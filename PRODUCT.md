# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

SaaS founders evaluating whether to hire a freelance AI integration specialist. They're technical enough to understand what Anthropic and OpenAI mean, but they're buying outcomes (shipped features, predictable cost, fast turnaround), not implementation details. They arrive from referrals, LinkedIn, or search, spend 60 seconds scanning, and either book a call or leave.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Kan, an AI integration specialist based in Tashkent. The site exists to convert a visitor's curiosity into a booked discovery call. Success: the visitor leaves thinking "this person ships real things, fast" and clicks the email CTA.

## Brand Personality

Technical, beautiful, creative.

The engineering rigor is real, but it manifests as craft and warmth, not coldness. The site should feel like meeting someone who builds extraordinary things and genuinely enjoys the work.

## References

- **fromanother.love**: dynamic visual flow, asymmetric balance, bold portfolio presence, numbered sections, photography-forward. The visitor feels the studio's capability through the design itself.
- **teatrlalka.pl**: warmth, color as personality, pictographic elements that feel human and inviting.
- **thatfestivalsite.com**: generous whitespace, typography-driven hierarchy, large hero moments.

What specifically resonates: **dynamic animations and warmth.**

## Anti-references

- **kawka.me**: ultra-restrained minimalism, grayscale, zero color experimentation, deliberately un-trendy. Too safe, too quiet.
- **gov.uk**: institutional austerity, utilitarian, function-only. No personality, no craft.

The pattern: anything that reads as "boring on purpose" or "restrained to the point of invisible." Safe is the enemy.

## Design Principles

1. **Craft is the proof.** The portfolio's execution is the pitch. If the site itself isn't beautifully engineered, the promise of beautifully engineered AI features rings hollow.
2. **Warm technical.** Engineering rigor expressed as beauty and care, not coldness or austerity. A human built this, and you can feel it.
3. **Commit to choices.** Bold palette, decisive typography, confident motion. Half-measures read as indecision. No hedging with safe neutrals.
4. **Motion as voice.** Animation isn't decoration; it's how the site communicates energy, competence, and attention to detail.
5. **Dynamic over static.** Asymmetry, rhythm, surprise. The opposite of a centered-stack template.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. The existing codebase already has solid foundations: skip links, ARIA labels, semantic HTML, prefers-reduced-motion throughout. Maintain and extend.
